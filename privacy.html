Privacy Policy

Natok Publisher — last updated 15 September 2026

Natok Publisher is operated by Cero Studio. It is a self-hosted tool that splits long-form Bangla drama videos into sequential parts, adds a text overlay, and publishes those parts to the operator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the application handles.

What we collect

  • TikTok account identifiers. When you connect your TikTok account, we receive your open ID, display name and avatar URL through TikTok's Login Kit.
  • Access and refresh tokens. Issued by TikTok so the application can publish on your behalf.
  • Video files and captions you supply. Provided by you through the application's own interface.

We do not collect your TikTok password. We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any kind.

How the data is used

Account identifiers and tokens are used for one purpose: authenticating with TikTok's Content Posting API to publish the video parts you have asked the application to publish. Video files are processed locally to cut them into parts and render text overlays.

Where the data is stored

Natok Publisher is self-hosted. All data — tokens, video files and processing state — is stored on the operator's own machine, in files readable only by the operating system account that runs the application (permission mode 600). Nothing is uploaded to servers controlled by Cero Studio, because no such servers exist in this application.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The only external service the application communicates with is TikTok itself, in order to publish your content, and Telegram, which is used as the operator's interface for submitting videos.

Retention and deletion

Source videos and generated parts are deleted automatically after publishing completes, by default within three days. Access tokens are kept only while your account remains connected. You can revoke access at any time from TikTok's own settings, under Security and permissions, which immediately invalidates the tokens held by this application. To have any remaining local data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data the application holds about you, and you may withdraw consent by disconnecting your TikTok account at any time.

Children

This application is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with an updated date.
